The Story

Man Vs Man

A Nottingham barber, a private suite, and a mission to give every client the best groom of their life.

The Beginning

From the Chair to the Suite

Mohammad picked up his first pair of clippers at 17 — not for a job, but because he loved it. He started cutting hair for friends and family, spending every spare hour watching tutorials, practising fades, and studying the craft with an intensity that went far beyond a hobby.

After completing his barbering qualifications, he started working in some of Nottingham's best-known shops. He was good — clients requested him specifically, waiting lists grew, and his eye for detail set him apart from the start. But something always felt off about the environment.

"Busy shops are chaotic," he says. "You're trying to give someone the best cut of their life while someone's shouting across the room, the next client is tapping their foot, and you're already running ten minutes late. I knew there was a better way."

The Private Suite

Man Vs Man Barbers was born from that frustration. A fully private, appointment-only suite designed around one principle: total focus on the client in the chair. No noise. No hustle. No compromise.

The suite was designed from the ground up with a premium aesthetic — black walls, warm lighting, curated music, and the smell of quality product in the air. Every detail was deliberate, because Mohammad knows that the experience starts the moment you walk through the door.

Since opening, the business has grown almost entirely through word of mouth. Clients travel from across the Midlands for appointments. The waiting list gets longer every month. And the products — developed from the same formulas Mohammad uses on every client — are now available for the first time online.

The Products

The Man Vs Man grooming range wasn't a business plan. It was a solution to a problem: clients kept asking what he used on their hair. After years of mixing, testing, and refining, the private-label range is everything he couldn't find off the shelf — the exact formulas, hold levels, and scents he'd always wanted.

Every product is used in every appointment. If it's not good enough for the suite, it's not good enough for the shop.
